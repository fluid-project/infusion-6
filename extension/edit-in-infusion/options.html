Edit in Infusion

GitHub

Your edits are saved to the site's repository on GitHub, as you. Sign in once, and the extension can save edits to any public repository you can write to. It keeps your sign-in in this browser's extension storage, where the pages you edit can't see it.

Signed in to GitHub.

Signing out forgets your sign-in in this browser. To withdraw the extension's access entirely, revoke "Edit in Infusion" in GitHub's settings for applications.

Sites

The extension does nothing on a site until you click its button there. Once you allow it access to a site, every page of the site shows the edits you have made but not yet seen published. Forgetting a site withdraws that access; the edits you hold for it are kept.
